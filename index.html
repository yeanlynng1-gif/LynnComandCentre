Working concept · Christmas 2026

A year-end pause with a practical way forward

Invite people to slow down, reflect on what this year has brought, and leave with one written intention and one small action for the next seven days. Mystic Blessings brings the spiritual space; Lynn brings the honest, useful guidance.

Up to 25 guestsTwo-hour draftTarot + meditation + sound + manifesting
The strategy

Make the experience easy to say yes to

  1. 01
    Lead with the result, then name the activities.Use “a calm year-end reset and a clear next step” as the main promise. Explain the tarot, meditation, sound bath, and manifesting underneath it. Avoid promising a prediction or guaranteed healing.
  2. 02
    Keep the first version manageable.Use a group tarot reflection for 25 guests. If you want personal readings, set a shorter guest list, add readers, or create a separate paid option.
  3. 03
    Set the price from costs and likely turnout.Plan with a realistic number of paying guests, not a full room. Include your own time and preparation in the earnings you want from the event.
  4. 04
    Make WhatsApp the booking desk.One clear event message, one QR payment step, one guest list, and one confirmation after you verify payment. Share updates with people who have asked to hear about the event.

Delivery plan

Two-hour run of show

Draft timings for a group of 25. Rehearse once and adjust for your venue.

00:00–00:10Welcome & settle

Check names against the paid guest list. Explain the flow, comfort options, and group boundaries.

00:10–00:25Guided meditation

Use one grounding prompt. Invite guests to sit or rest in the way that works for them.

00:25–01:00Group tarot reflection

Choose a shared theme, draw cards, then give each guest a written reflection prompt.

01:00–01:20Short sound bath

Test the room and sound level before guests arrive. Give a quiet seating option.

01:20–01:50Manifest & make it real

Guests write one intention, one action for the next seven days, and what might get in the way.

01:50–02:00Close & connect

Invite a voluntary share, explain follow-up, and let guests leave without feeling rushed.

Before the event

Promotion plan

Now: decide the offer

Lock date, venue, format, group tarot approach, costs, price, and cancellation terms. Then write one short invitation with who it is for, what happens, and how to book.

About four weeks out: open bookings

Start with people who have asked for event updates and your existing content channels. Share the experience, the practical takeaway, and the booking steps. Track enquiries and paid places separately.

Weekly: show a piece of the experience

Use one post or message each week: a reflection prompt, a short introduction to the sound bath, a look at the venue, or an explanation of the tarot group format. Answer real questions you receive.

Ten days out: review bookings

Compare paid places with your minimum viable turnout. Decide whether to increase outreach, simplify costs, or make another clear call to action.

On the ground

Booking & delivery checklist

Booking SOP

Enquiry → send confirmed details → send QR → check payment → add name to guest list → send confirmation. Record dietary or access needs only if relevant to your venue and with care.

Three days before

Reconcile paid names, send time, map or link, arrival instructions, and what to bring. Prepare cards, instruments, pens, paper, water, and a timer.

Event day

Arrive early to test sound, arrange seating, set up check-in, and walk through the transitions. Keep the run sheet in front of you; leave a little buffer.

Within two days after

Send a warm thank-you and one practical reflection question. Note attendance, costs, common questions, and what to improve next time.
